# HLA Requirements Lab document status

These are HLA Requirements Lab source compilations: independently assembled, edited, and published by the Lab for requirements analysis and interoperability work.

The Lab-authored layer includes the cover, branding, editorial compilation, semantic model, traceability, quality gates, and annotations.

The documents consolidate supplied HLA 1516-family source material. The 2010 and 2025 labels identify the source set used for each compilation; they do not present a Lab edition as a separately issued external standard.

The Lab does not assign these editions to IEEE or describe IEEE as their publisher, author, owner, approver, or endorser. Source identifiers are retained only for traceability, and any external use of source material should be evaluated independently.

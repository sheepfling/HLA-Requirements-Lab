\clause{4. Summary of the HLA rules}

The rules for federations are as follows:

\begin{hlarules}
    \item Federations shall have an HLA FOM, documented in accordance with the HLA OMT.
    \item In a federation, all simulation-associated object instance representation shall be in the\linebreak
    federates, not in the RTI.
    \item During a federation execution, all exchange of FOM data among joined federates shall occur\linebreak
    via the RTI.
    \item During a federation execution, joined federates shall interact with the RTI in accordance with\linebreak
    the HLA interface specification.
    \item During a federation execution, an instance attribute shall be owned by at most one joined\linebreak
    federate at any given time.
\end{hlarules}

The rules for federates are as follows:

\begin{hlarules}
    \setcounter{enumi}{5}
    \item Federates shall have an HLA SOM, documented in accordance with the HLA OMT.
    \item Federates shall be able to update and/or reflect any instance attributes and send and/or receive\linebreak
    interactions, as specified in their SOMs.
    \item Federates shall be able to transfer and/or accept ownership of instance attributes dynamically\linebreak
    during a federation execution, as specified in their SOMs.
    \item Federates shall be able to vary the conditions (e.g., thresholds) under which they provide\linebreak
    updates of instance attributes, as specified in their SOMs.
    \item Federates shall be able to manage local time in a way that will allow them to coordinate data\linebreak
    exchange with other members of a federation.
\end{hlarules}

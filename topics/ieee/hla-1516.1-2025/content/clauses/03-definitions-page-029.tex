  \HLADefinitionAt{103.81}{%
    \HLADefinitionAnchor{optimistic-logical-time}\HLADefinitionTerm{optimistic logical time (OLT)}: A supplementary logical time that facilitates optimistic time advancement.\linebreak
    The optimistic logical time is provided, along with the update to the federate's logical time, in a \emph{Flush Queue}\linebreak
    \emph{Grant \textdagger{}} service call as a response to a \emph{Flush Queue Request} service call. It is the minimum of the time\linebreak
    supplied in the \emph{Flush Queue Request} and the timestamps of any TSO messages delivered in response to that\linebreak
    \emph{Flush Queue Request}. The optimistic logical time indicates a lower bound on the next logical time the\linebreak
    federate could be advanced to if no TSO messages with a timestamp less than it are received in the future.%
  }

  \HLADefinitionAt{187.80}{%
    \HLADefinitionAnchor{optimistic-lookahead}\HLADefinitionTerm{optimistic lookahead}: The lookahead the federate would have if actually granted to the optimistic logical\linebreak
    time.%
  }

    federates that are delivered to a single joined federate. Different categories of service are defined with\linebreak
    different characteristics regarding whether and how an RTI orders messages that are to be delivered to a\linebreak
    joined federate.%

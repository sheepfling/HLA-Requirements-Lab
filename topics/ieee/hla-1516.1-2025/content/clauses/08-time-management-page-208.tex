  }
  \HLAFixedBlock{90.001}{316.805}{431.999}{\rmfamily\raggedright}{9.96}{12.00}{%
    shall be greater than or equal to zero. If the requested value is greater than or equal to the joined federate’s%

  \HLAFixedBlock{90.000}{340.805}{432.000}{\rmfamily\raggedright}{9.96}{12.00}{%

  }
  \HLAFixedBlock{90.000}{352.805}{432.000}{\rmfamily\raggedright}{9.96}{12.00}{%

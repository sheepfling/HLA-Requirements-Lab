    \item Supplied arguments: arguments supplied to the service
    \item Returned arguments: arguments returned by the service.
    \item Preconditions: conditions that shall exist for the service to execute correctly.
    \item Postconditions: conditions that shall exist once the service has executed correctly.
    \item Exceptions: notifications of any irregularity that may occur during service invocation. Wherever\linebreak

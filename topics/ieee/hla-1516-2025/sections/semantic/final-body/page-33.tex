\HLADetailedRuleHeading{detailed-rule-2}{5.2 Rule 2}
\vspace{12.79pt}

\HLANormativeRuleStatement{In a federation, all simulation-associated object instance representation shall be in the federates, not in the\linebreak RTI.}
\vspace{11.81pt}

In the HLA, the responsibility for maintaining the values of HLA object instance attributes shall take place\linebreak
in the joined federate. In an HLA federation, all joined federate-associated instance attributes shall be\linebreak
owned by federates, not by the RTI. However, the RTI may own instance attributes associated with the\linebreak
federation MOM. The RTI may use data about instance attributes and interactions to support RTI services\linebreak
(e.g., Declaration Management), but these data are merely used by the RTI, not changed.\par
\vspace{24.96pt}

\HLADetailedRuleHeading{detailed-rule-3}{5.3 Rule 3}
\vspace{12.79pt}

\HLANormativeRuleStatement{During a federation execution, all exchange of FOM data among joined federates shall occur via the RTI.}
\vspace{11.81pt}

The HLA federate interface specification (see IEEE Std 1516.1-2025) specifies a set of interfaces to\linebreak
services in the RTI to support coordinated exchange of instance attribute values and interactions in\linebreak
accordance with a federation’s FOM. Under the HLA, intercommunication of FOM data among joined\linebreak
federates participating in a given federation execution shall be executed by the exchange of data via the\linebreak
RTI services. Based on the FOM, joined federates shall identify to the RTI what information they will\linebreak
provide and require, along with instance attribute and interaction data corresponding to the changing state\linebreak
of object instances in the joined federate. The RTI shall then provide the coordination, synchronization, and\linebreak
data exchange among the joined federates to permit a coherent execution of the federation.\par
\vspace{11.94pt}

Ensuring that the right data are provided at the right times and that the data are used in a substantively\linebreak
correct way shall be the responsibility of the joined federates. The RTI shall ensure that the data are\linebreak
delivered using joined federates in accordance with their declared requirements (e.g., which data, reliability\linebreak
of transport, event ordering) to provide a common view of shared data across a given federation execution,\linebreak
as specified in the FOM.\par
\vspace{11.94pt}

RTI services shall be used to ensure that the coordination needs of the distributed applications (federations)\linebreak
are met consistently across all participants in a federation and over the life of a federation execution.\par
\vspace{24.96pt}

\HLADetailedRuleHeading{detailed-rule-4}{5.4 Rule 4}
\vspace{12.79pt}

\HLANormativeRuleStatement{During a federation execution, joined federates shall interact with the RTI in accordance with the HLA\linebreak interface specification.}
\vspace{11.81pt}

The HLA provides a specification for a standard interface between the federate application and the RTI.\linebreak
Joined federates shall use this standard interface to access RTI services (see IEEE Std 1516.1-2025). The\linebreak
specification shall define how federate applications interact with the infrastructure. However, because the\linebreak
interface and the RTI will be used for a wide variety of applications requiring data exchange of diverse\linebreak
characteristics, the interface specification says nothing about the specific federate data to be exchanged\linebreak
over the interface.\par

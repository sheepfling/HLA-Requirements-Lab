\HLAFrontHeading{Use by artificial intelligence systems}
\vspace{12.62pt}

In no event shall material in any IEEE Standards documents be used for the purpose of creating, training,\linebreak
enhancing, developing, maintaining, or contributing to any artificial intelligence systems without the\linebreak
express, written consent of IEEE SA in advance. “Artificial intelligence” refers to any software,\linebreak
application, or other system that uses artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar technologies, to\linebreak
analyze, train, process, or generate content. Requests for consent can be submitted using the Contact Us\linebreak
form.\par

\HLAFrontHeading{Official statements}
\vspace{12.68pt}

A statement, written or oral, that is not processed in accordance with the IEEE SA Standards Board\linebreak
Operations Manual is not, and shall not be considered or inferred to be, the official position of IEEE or any\linebreak
of its committees and shall not be considered to be, or be relied upon as, a formal position of IEEE or IEEE\linebreak
SA. At lectures, symposia, seminars, or educational courses, an individual presenting information on IEEE\linebreak
standards shall make it clear that the presenter’s views should be considered the personal views of that\linebreak
individual rather than the formal position of IEEE, IEEE SA, the Standards Committee, or the Working\linebreak
Group. Statements made by volunteers may not represent the formal position of their employer(s) or\linebreak
affiliation(s). News releases about IEEE standards issued by entities other than IEEE SA should be\linebreak
{\addfontfeatures{FakeStretch=0.985}considered the view of the entity issuing the release rather than the formal position of IEEE or IEEE SA.}\par

\HLAFrontHeading{Comments on standards}
\vspace{12.69pt}

Comments for revision of IEEE Standards documents are welcome from any interested party, regardless of\linebreak
membership affiliation with IEEE or IEEE SA. However, \textbf{IEEE does not provide interpretations,}\linebreak
\textbf{consulting information, or advice pertaining to IEEE Standards documents}.\par
\vspace{12.11pt}

Suggestions for changes in documents should be in the form of a proposed change of text, together with\linebreak
appropriate supporting comments. Since IEEE standards represent a consensus of concerned interests, it is\linebreak
important that any responses to comments and questions also receive the concurrence of a balance of\linebreak
interests. For this reason, IEEE and the members of its Societies and subcommittees of the IEEE SA Board\linebreak
of Governors are not able to provide an instant response to comments or questions, except in those cases\linebreak
where the matter has previously been addressed. For the same reason, IEEE does not respond to\linebreak
interpretation requests. Any person who would like to participate in evaluating comments or revisions to an\linebreak
IEEE standard is welcome to join the relevant IEEE SA working group. You can indicate interest in a\linebreak
working group using the Interests tab in the Manage Profile \& Interests area of the \textcolor{blue}{IEEE SA myProject}\linebreak
\textcolor{blue}{system}.\textsuperscript{1} An IEEE Account is needed to access the application.\par
\vspace{12.22pt}

Comments on standards should be submitted using the \textcolor{blue}{Contact Us} form.\textsuperscript{2}\par

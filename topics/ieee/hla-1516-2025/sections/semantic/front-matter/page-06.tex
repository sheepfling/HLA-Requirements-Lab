\HLAFrontHeading{Updating of IEEE Standards documents}
\vspace{12.68pt}

Users of IEEE Standards documents should be aware that these documents may be superseded at any time\linebreak
by the issuance of new editions or may be amended from time to time through the issuance of amendments,\linebreak
corrigenda, or errata. An official IEEE document at any point in time consists of the current edition of the\linebreak
document together with any amendments, corrigenda, or errata then in effect.\par
\vspace{12.10pt}

Every IEEE standard is subjected to review at least every 10 years. When a document is more than 10 years\linebreak
old and has not undergone a revision process, it is reasonable to conclude that its contents, although still of\linebreak
some value, do not wholly reflect the present state of the art. Users are cautioned to check to determine that\linebreak
they have the latest edition of any IEEE standard.\par
